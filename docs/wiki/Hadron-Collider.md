# Olympian Hadron Collider (`5×5`)

## 1. Ring Assembly
- `1× Collider Controller`
- `12× Ring Coils` (minimum `9`)
- **Required Ports:** `1× Energy Port`, `1× Item Port`, `1× Fluid Port`
- **Inner Ring:** `3×3` center must remain **Air**.
- Supports `[Auto-Build]`, `[Holo 5×5]`, and up to **5M RF/t** port I/O. Item Ports & Coils auto-output into Modular Chests and pipes.

> **Danger:** Entering the active `3×3` core deals heavy radiation damage!

## 2. Cryo Cooling & Thermal Quench
- **`< 120 K` Superconducting:** `1.5× RF` output & `2.5×` acceleration!
- **`>= 650 K` Thermal Quench:** Halts acceleration (Max `800 K`).
- **Coolants:** Bio-Ethanol (`2× Cryo`), Water, Ice / Blue Ice.
- **SYNCHROTRON Mode:** Locks max speed for steady RF generation.
- **COLLIDER Mode:** Press **`[SMASH!]`** at high speed for particle collisions (`2.5× RF` bursts + rare `10%` drops).

## 3. Plasma Fuel Multipliers
- **Pyro-Diesel:** `2.5×` RF & Velocity
- **Distillate:** `1.8×`
- **Biodiesel:** `1.4×`
- **Bio-Oil / Lava:** `1.0×` Baseline
