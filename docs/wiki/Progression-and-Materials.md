# Progression & Materials

Obsidium expands Minecraft's mid-to-late game progression around **Volcanite** and volcanic metallurgy.

## Core Resources
- **Volcanite Ore** → drops **Volcanite** (can be crushed into **Volcanite Dust** in the Volcanite Grinder).
- **Volcanic Ash** & **Volcanic Alloy** → early/mid-game alloy components used for casings, pipes, and basic machines.
- **Volcanite Ingot** → fire-resistant core ingot used across advanced machines, wires, and **Mythic Rails** hardware.
- **Volcanic Plate** & **Volcanite Binding** → structural crafting parts for tools, armor, and industrial frames.

## Equipment Tiers
1. **Basic Volcanic Set** (`Volcanic Sword`, `Pickaxe`, `Axe`, `Shovel`, `Hoe` + Full Armor)
2. **Advanced Volcanic Set** (`Advanced Volcanic Sword`, `Pickaxe`, `Axe`, `Shovel`, `Hoe` + Full Armor)

> **Tip:** Use the **Magma Forge Manual** in-game to inspect forge recipes, heat mechanics, and **Tartarus Piston** cooling behavior.
