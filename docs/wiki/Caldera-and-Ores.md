# Caldera & Volcanite Ores

![Volcanite Ore](assets/icons/volcanite_ore.png) ![Volcanite](assets/icons/volcanite.png) ![Volcanite Ingot](assets/icons/volcanite_ingot.png)

## 1. Caldera Biome (Overworld)
- Hot, arid volcanic plateaus and highlands made of terraced Smooth Basalt & Blackstone.
- **Pure Overworld:** No Nether or End portals required to begin Obsidium progression.
- **Telluric Astrolabe:** Scans for Caldera biomes up to **6.4 km** away.

## 2. Volcanic Cones
- Surface basalt vents with active magma craters.
- Rich **Volcanite Ore** deposits encrust the slopes — mine Volcanite directly on the surface and safely scoop Lava from crater vents.

## 3. Volcanite Mining
- **Tool Tier:** Mines with an **Iron Pickaxe+** (no Diamond required).
- **Drops:** Volcanite crystals (boosted by **Fortune**); **Silk Touch** drops raw ore intact.
- **Vein Locations:**
  - **Surface:** Volcanic Cones in Caldera biomes.
  - **Underground:** Thermal veins near Lava pools.
  - **Deepslate:** Deep veins between `Y: -64` and `Y: -10`.
  - **Nether:** Abundant near lava seas and basalt deltas.
