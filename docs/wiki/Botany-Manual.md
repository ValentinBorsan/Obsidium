# Demetrian Botany Manual

[Botany item catalog — images, recipes and acquisition routes](wiki.html?page=Botany-Catalog)

This practical guide accompanies the in-game Demetrian Botany Book. Craft the book from its catalog recipe, then right-click while holding it to open its chapters. The Magma Forge Manual is also listed in the catalog and opens the general Obsidium guide.

## Resource Crops

Craft the seed for the resource you want to grow, plant it on supported soil (including farmland) and let the crop mature. Harvested petals supply resource-conversion recipes. The catalog separates seed crafting from crop-harvest routes; immature and mature drops follow the corresponding crop loot table.

## Grafting Step by Step

1. Craft a Demetrian Grafting Knife, Grafting Tape and Botanist's Lens.
2. Sneak and right-click supported leaves with the knife to obtain species-bearing scion cuttings.
3. Plant a rootstock sapling, then right-click it with the knife to incise it.
4. Apply a compatible scion to the incised rootstock.
5. Bind with Grafting Tape to trigger metamorphosis.
6. Use the Botanist's Lens to inspect the graft. Grow the resulting sapling as its species requires.

A Demetrian Scion is one item with species data, rather than a separate registered item for every species. The catalog shows its generic icon. Hybrid results are gameplay operations, not crafting-table recipes.

## Hybrid Examples

- Ares Iron + Hades Coal produces Astrape Redstone.
- Midas Gold + Hermes Copper produces Poseidon Lapis.
- Midas Gold + Artemis Silver produces Electrum.
- Ares Iron + Artemis Silver produces Apollo Quartz.
- Astrape Redstone + Apollo Quartz produces Dionysian Amethyst.
- Astrape Redstone + Midas Gold produces Hyperion Glowstone.
- Poseidon Lapis + Electrum or Midas Gold produces Plouton Emerald.
- Tartarean Netherite + Plouton Emerald produces Adamantine Diamond.

These pairs follow GraftingRegistry. Use the matching species on rootstock and scion; the registry also supports reversed pairs. Do not assume an arbitrary pair creates a new species.

## Rare Trees and Pyretic Titan

Rare-tree renewal requires an existing rare specimen. Netherite cannot be bootstrapped from common trees alone. During graft metamorphosis, a Tartarean Netherite or Adamantine Diamond rootstock/scion enables a 5% Pyretic Titan mutation roll. This is a chance, not a guaranteed hybrid result. Grow the resulting Pyretic Titan Sapling in a Demetrian Bonsai Planter to obtain Pyretic Titan logs and leaves.

## Fruit and Automation

Ripe mineral-tree leaves supply species-specific fruit. The Geothermal Arborist can harvest ripe fruiting leaves. Bonsai Planters offer compact tree production; the Lumber Depot and Sapling Barrel support the botanical production chain. Their crafting recipes are available in the catalog.

## Volcanic Beekeeping

- Wait until the Volcanic Beehive is full.
- Use shears to harvest three Volcanic Honeycombs.
- Use a glass bottle to obtain a Volcanic Honey Bottle.
- Place a lit campfire below the hive to smoke it before harvesting and avoid angering bees.

The hive, honey products and Pollinating Brush are listed with their crafting or harvesting routes.
