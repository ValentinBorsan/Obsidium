# Magma Forge

[Multiblock construction guide — layers, blocks and formation](wiki.html?page=Multiblocks)

## 3×3 Magma Forge Blueprint
Build a flat **3×3** platform:
- **Center:** `1× Forge Core`
- **Sides:** `4× Casings`
- **Corners:** `4× Energy Cells / Units`

> Right-click the center **Forge Core** to form the multiblock — the 8 outer blocks become active forge casings.

## Magma Tempering
- **HIT `[SPACE]` IN GREEN** during the tempering bar to trigger a **Perfect Strike**:
  - `+35%` Forging Progress & `+1 ★` quality!
- **Masterwork (`2+ ★`):**
  - `-50%` material/energy cost **+ Bonus Output Item!**
- **1-Click Auto-Fill:** Click any recipe in the Forge UI to automatically pull ingredients from your inventory/network.
