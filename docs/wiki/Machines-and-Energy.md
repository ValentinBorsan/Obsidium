The catalog covers processing machines, generators, pumps, botanical automation and craftable multiblock components. Crafting grids show how to build each block; operating recipes and multiblock assembly are separate topics.

Obsidium uses **RF / Team Reborn Energy** (`teamreborn:energy`) for machines, wireless power, and rail integration.

## Processing & Generation
- **Basic / Advanced Volcanite Furnace** — High-speed ore and item smelting.
- **Basic / Advanced Volcanite Forge** — Multi-ingredient alloy forging.
- **Volcanite Grinder** — Crushes ores into dusts (`Iron Dust`, `Gold Dust`, `Copper Dust`, `Volcanite Dust`) to multiply yields.
- **Volcanite Basic Generator** — Generates RF from solid/volcanic fuels.

## Energy Storage & Transmission
- **Basic Volcanite Energy Cell** / **Advanced Volcanite Energy Cell** — High-capacity RF storage blocks.
- **Astrape Energy Wire** (`Basic`, `Advanced`, `Supreme`) — Transfers RF between generators, cells, machines, and **Mythic Rails** energy stations.
  - *Auto-Source Detection*: Wires automatically pull from energy sources (blocks with `supportsInsertion() == false`, such as Generators and `Hermes Energy Unloading Station`).
- **Hephaestean Induction Charging Plate** — Charges held/inventory energy items and equipment.
- **Astrape Wireless Energy Pylon** — Wireless RF distribution across nearby machines.
