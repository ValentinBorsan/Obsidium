## Generators
- **Basic Volcanite Generator** — Burns solid/volcanic fuels for early RF generation.
- **Geothermal Generator** — Consumes Lava (`10 mB/s`) with passive + lava adjacency boost.
- **Steam Turbine** — Converts `Water + Lava ➔ Steam` into continuous RF power.
- **Supreme Volcanic Generator** — High-output pyroclastic eruption generator producing up to **2,500 RF/t**.
- **Demetrian Biomass Generator** — Converts harvested Demetrian fruit into biogas + energy (up to **800 RF/t**).

## Multiblocks & Thermal Chemistry
- **Tartarus Piston** — Heavy geothermal power/cooling multiblock.
- **Bio-Caldera** — Biological thermal reactor.
- **Fluid Alchemy & Synthesis** (100% passive thermal automation with zero fuel):
  - **Crucible:** Produces Lava
  - **Condenser:** Produces Water
  - **Vitrifier:** Produces Obsidian
  - **Lithofier:** Produces Rocks
- **Catalysts & Fuels:** Astral Catalyst (`4×` in Forge) and Pyroclastic Fuel (`1,000 mB` batches).
