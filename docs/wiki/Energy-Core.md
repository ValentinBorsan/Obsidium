## 1. 3×3 Energy Core Matrix
- **Center:** `1× Energy Core`
- **Ring (8 blocks):** `4–8× Energy Cells / Units` + `0–4× Casings` (auto-forms with 8 valid ring blocks).

### Scaling Capacities
- **Basic Volcanite Energy Cell:** `1×1` = `2M RF` (`25k RF/t`) | In `3×3` = `+25M RF` per cell.
- **Advanced Volcanite Energy Cell:** `1×1` = `15M RF` (`150k RF/t`) | In `3×3` = `+100M RF` per cell.

---

## 2. Keraunos HV Matrix (`3×3×3`)
- **Tier:** High-Voltage (`1.0B – 15.0B+ RF`) — complements the 3×3 Core!
- **Structure:** `1× Keraunos HV Core` at the exact center `(Y=0)`, surrounded by `26` shell blocks across Bottom (`Y-1`), Middle (`Y=0`), and Top (`Y+1`).
- **Valid Shell Blocks:** Casings, Volcanite Glass, Energy Cells/Units, and **at least `1× HV Port`**.
- **Capacity:** `1.0 BRF` base (`+150M RF` per Basic Cell / `+600M RF` per Adv Unit).

## 3. High-Voltage Safety Protocols
- **Aether-Faraday Suit (Full `4/4` Set Required):**
  - Blocks proximity HV arcs (`4.5–6.5m` radius).
  - Grants **100% Lightning Immunity**.
- **HV Dielectric Hot-Stick:** Required for safe manual interaction with energized HV ports.
