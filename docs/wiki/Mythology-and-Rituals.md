## 1. Olympian Altar Multiblock
- **Center:** `1× Olympian Altar` at `(0, 0)`
- **Pylons:** `4× Astrape Ritual Obelisks` placed at **North, South, East, West** at **EXACTLY 3 blocks distance**, all on the **same Y level**.
- **Usage:** Place a Divine Scroll on an Obelisk and the target Item on the Altar. **Punch (Left-Click)** the Altar to retrieve the infused weapon!

## 2. The 7 Divine Pacts (Limit: 1 per Weapon)
- **Zeus:** Chain Lightning arcs (`4.0` damage to up to 3 foes).
- **Apollo:** Celestial fire (`3.5` true damage, ignores armor).
- **Artemis:** `+40%` attack speed & critical strike power.
- **Athena:** `35%` incoming damage nullify chance.
- **Hephaestus:** Auto-Smelt mined drops & targets.
- **Poseidon:** `+6.0` bonus water/trident damage.
- **Thanatos:** Soul Siphon (`+2.0` hearts healed on kill).

## 3. Mythic Relics & Ritual Tech
- **Bow of Artemis:** Forged with Silver Ingots & Artemis Silver Fruit (`+3.0` base damage, `+40%` arrow speed, infinite ammo while carrying Silver Fruit, piercing moonlight vs demons/undead).
- **Aegis of Athena:** `1,600` durability fire-resistant shield that reflects projectiles/spells and triggers a volcanic shockwave on melee attackers.
- **Apollo Helio-Concentrator & Distillery:** Solar ritual energy and mythic brewing.
