# Mythology & Rituals

Obsidium weaves Greek mythology into late-game crafting and combat progression:

- **Olympian Altar** — Central ritual block for mythic crafting and blessings.
- **Astrape Ritual Obelisk** — Ritual pylon placed around the Olympian Altar to channel lightning/energy.
- **Hephaestean Armory** — Specialized smithing station for mythic gear and upgrades.
