# Obsidium

**Build a volcanic workshop. Automate your resources. Reach Olympian industry.**

Obsidium combines volcanic technology, automation and mythology in a Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21. Start with a workshop and develop it into an interconnected industrial base.

## A world shaped by fire

Explore the Volcanic Caldera and bring its resources into your workshop.

- Volcanic materials, mineral-bearing trees and crops connect exploration with production.
- Mythology gives the machines, equipment and progression a shared identity.

## Machines with a purpose

Turn raw materials into the foundations of your factory.

- Smelt, grind, distill and process resources with volcanic machinery.
- Expand into advanced alloys, specialized equipment and industrial multiblocks.

## Power, transport & storage

Connect the workshop into an automated production line.

- Generate and distribute energy with generators, wires and energy storage.
- Move items and fluids with Hermes and Naiad logistics; expand storage with modular chests and Quantum Link infrastructure.

## From workshop to late-game industry

Progress through thermal machinery, automation and demanding late-game technology.

- Early game: establish processing and build your first workshop.
- Mid game: automate materials, power and transport.
- Late game: tackle Supreme technology, the Olympian Hadron Collider and rare materials.

## Guidance as you build

Use the in-game manuals, Odyssey advancements and online wiki to discover systems and milestones.

- Follow progression goals while choosing which parts of the workshop to expand.
- Use configuration options to adjust supported systems for your world or modpack.

## Explore the wiki & item catalog

Find what you need before you build: the wiki brings together **405 core items across 10 categories**, with game images, visual crafting recipes and acquisition methods.

- **Browse by system:** materials, machines, pipes, storage, botany, energy, equipment, building blocks, mythology and supplies.
- **Craft at the right station:** recipe labels identify when a **Volcanite Worktable** is needed instead of the vanilla crafting table.
- **Build multiblocks layer by layer:** follow illustrated layouts, component lists and formation instructions.
- **Grow your resources:** use the Botany Manual for grafting, living-tree hybrids, rare mutations and beekeeping.

## Before you install

- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21
- Java 21
- Fabric Loader and Fabric API for Minecraft 1.21
- Install Obsidium on both the client and the server.

REI is optional. Team Reborn Energy is bundled.

**Compatibility note:** targeted Tech Reborn item, fluid and energy checks have passed. This does not establish compatibility with every machine or modpack.
