# 🗺️ Obsidium: Olympian Odyssey — Quest Progression Guide

This guide outlines the complete 4-chapter narrative questline designed for **Heracles** or **FTB Quests**.

> [!NOTE]
> The questline ships **built into the mod** as a native advancement tab (**Obsidium: Olympian Odyssey**, press `L` in-game) — no quest mod required.
> Files: `src/main/resources/data/obsidium/advancement/odyssey/*.json`. Item quests complete automatically when items enter your inventory; the ritual quests (13 & 14) are granted by the Olympian Altar to every player within 24 blocks when a ritual completes. Each quest rewards XP (25 → 1000).

---

### 🌋 Chapter I: The Ash Age
* **Quest 1: The Spark of Vulcan**
  * *Description*: Dig deep into volcanic crusts to gather raw volcanic ash and compact it into combustible briquettes.
  * *Objective*: Collect 16x `obsidium:volcanic_ash` and 4x `obsidium:ash_briquette`.
* **Quest 2: Mechanical Pulverization**
  * *Description*: Craft your first Volcanite Grinder to crush raw ores into mineral dust, yielding double metal output.
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:volcanite_grinder`.
* **Quest 3: Magmatic Smelting**
  * *Description*: Forge your first batch of refined Volcanite Ingots and Volcanic Alloy plates.
  * *Objective*: Obtain 4x `obsidium:volcanite_ingot` and 2x `obsidium:volcanic_alloy`.

---

### ⚡ Chapter II: The Volcanic Grid & Automation
* **Quest 4: Geothermal & Steam Power**
  * *Description*: Harness geothermal gradients or boiling steam to produce steady Redstone Flux (RF).
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:volcanite_steam_generator` or `obsidium:volcanite_geothermal_generator`.
* **Quest 5: Grid Infrastructure**
  * *Description*: Lay down insulated power conduits and logistical pipes to distribute energy and items seamlessly throughout your base.
  * *Objective*: Craft 16x `obsidium:volcanite_storage_pipe` or `obsidium:volcanite_energy_wire`.
* **Quest 6: The Digital Nexus**
  * *Description*: Set up a centralized Storage Controller paired with a Long-Range Wireless Remote for instant storage access up to 256 blocks away.
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:volcanite_storage_controller` and 1x `obsidium:volcanite_storage_remote`.
* **Quest 7: Hephaestean Induction**
  * *Description*: Construct a wireless induction pad to rapidly recharge portable battery accumulators and active inventory tools.
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:hephaestean_charging_plate` and 1x `obsidium:volcanite_portable_battery`.

---

### 🍇 Chapter III: Sacred Dionysian Orchards
* **Quest 8: Harvest of the Gods**
  * *Description*: Cultivate metallic-stem flora and press sacred fruit yields to extract precious golden nuggets and rich pomace.
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:dionysian_press` and obtain 8x `obsidium:dionysian_pomace`.
* **Quest 9: The Alchemical Still**
  * *Description*: Refine sacred pomace mash inside the Dionysian Distillery to brew Volcanic Țuică and Spiced Cider.
  * *Objective*: Brew 1x `obsidium:volcanic_tuica` and 1x `obsidium:volcanic_cider`.
* **Quest 10: The Grand Reserve**
  * *Description*: Age your spirit inside a Dionysian Aging Barrel to mature it into an exalted Vintage Volcanic Țuică.
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:dionysian_aging_barrel` and age 1x `obsidium:vintage_volcanic_tuica`.

---

### 🏛️ Chapter IV: Mount Olympus & Divine Ascension
* **Quest 11: The Olympian High Altar**
  * *Description*: Erect the Olympian Altar surrounded symmetrically by 4 Astrape Ritual Obelisks spaced 3 blocks away (North, South, East, West).
  * *Objective*: Craft 1x `obsidium:olympian_altar` and 4x `obsidium:astrape_ritual_obelisk`.
* **Quest 12: Sacred Inscriptions**
  * *Description*: Forge Volcanic Papyrus sheets and uncover ancient Mythological Inscription Scrolls.
  * *Objective*: Craft 4x `obsidium:volcanic_papyrus` and obtain a divine scroll (e.g. `obsidium:scroll_of_zeus`).
* **Quest 13: Wrath of the Thunderer**
  * *Description*: Place a Hephaestean weapon upon the altar and the Scroll of Zeus upon an obelisk during a raging thunderstorm to invoke the wrath of the heavens.
  * *Objective*: Infuse any weapon with the legendary `Pact of Zeus`.
* **Quest 14: Olympian Mastery**
  * *Description*: Master all 7 celestial phenomena (Zeus's Storm, Apollo's Noon Sun, Artemis's Moonlit Hunt, Athena's Bastion, Hephaestus's Core Flame, Poseidon's Tempest, and Thanatos's Underworld Chasm).
  * *Reward*: The Grand Demigod of Olympus Trophy & Title.
