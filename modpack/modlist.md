# 📦 Obsidium: Olympian Odyssey — Modpack Specification
**Minecraft Version:** `1.21`  
**Mod Loader:** `Fabric Loader >= 0.16.5`  
**Target Performance:** `144+ FPS, 4-6 GB RAM allocated`

---

## 🏛️ Core Mod
* **Obsidium** (`obsidium-1.0.0.jar`)
  * Volcanic machines, magmatic forge, fluid/item pipe routing, and wireless digital storage network.
  * Dionysian mythological crops, metal-bearing flora, sacred winemaking, pressing, and distillation.
  * The Olympian Altar with 4 Astrape Obelisks and 7 exclusive Divine Pact enchantments.

---

## ⚡ Performance & Optimization Engine (Essential)
These mods guarantee 120–200+ FPS even on modest setups:
1. **Sodium** (`fabric`) — Ground-up overhaul of the modern OpenGL graphics engine for massive FPS increases.
2. **Lithium** (`fabric`) — Deep optimizations for entity physics, mob AI, and world chunk ticking.
3. **FerriteCore** (`fabric`) — Drastically reduces RAM consumption by 35–50%.
4. **ImmediatelyFast** (`fabric`) — Accelerates rendering of text, containers, and HUD/GUI components.
5. **ModernFix** (`fabric`) — Fixes memory leaks, structural bugs, and significantly speeds up game launch times.
6. **Entity Culling** (`fabric`) — Skips rendering of entities blocked by walls or solid blocks.

---

## 🔍 UI, Recipes & Quality of Life (QoL)
1. **Roughly Enough Items (REI)** or **EMI** — Built-in recipe viewer support for Grinder, Magma Forge, Dionysian Distillery, Press, Aging Barrel, and Olympian Altar rituals.
2. **Jade** (What Am I Looking At) — Native Obsidium plugin: RF energy, fluid tanks, machine processing progress (% and seconds left), Olympian Altar offering/ritual progress, and obelisk scrolls.
3. **Mod Menu** — Clean in-game interface for viewing and configuring installed mods.
4. **AppleSkin** — Visualizes food value, hunger restoration, and saturation for all Dionysian beverages and mythic fruits.

---

## 🌍 World Generation & Atmosphere (Volcanic Aesthetics & Mount Olympus)
1. **Terralith** (`fabric`) — Adds 85+ spectacular biomes (colossal peaks, volcanic calderas, deep magma caverns) without adding un-vanilla blocks.
2. **Tectonic** (`fabric`) — Amplifies mountain heights and valley depths, delivering an epic "Mount Olympus" terrain feel.

---

## ⚔️ Mythological Combat & Equipment
1. **Better Combat** (`fabric`) — Seamless fluid weapon attack animations, combos, and sweep trajectories tailored for Hephaestean Greatswords, Paxels, and Artemis's Bow.
2. **Accessories** or **Trinkets** (`fabric`) — Dedicated equipment slots for Hermes's Winged Sandals (Talaria), the Eternal Coin, and the Dionysian Thyrsus Staff.

---

## 📜 Quest Progression System
* **Heracles** or **FTB Quests** — Guides the player step-by-step from the primitive Ash Age to automated Volcanic Industry, Sacred Dionysian Vineyards, and Divine Demigod Ascension on Mount Olympus.
